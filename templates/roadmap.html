Live now

Fleet visibility

One view of every depot, every vehicle and every claim against your real fleet register and loss ratio — this portal, backed by a real database.

Overview, Portfolio, Claims and Fleet Performance all read from the same live vehicle and claims register. Explorer now adds a depot-by-depot view of that same real data. No spreadsheets to maintain, no data re-keyed by hand.

*
Next

Telematics integration

Connecting your fleet's telematics feed alongside claims data — harsh braking, speed, mileage, time of day — and linking specific vehicles to the claims recorded against them.

Claims history tells us what's already gone wrong. Telematics tells us what's happening on the road right now. Bringing the two together, and down to individual vehicle level, is what turns this from a report into an early-warning system.

The vision

Driver risk intelligence

Every employed and self-employed driver, risk-profiled from telematics, claims and premium data combined — with a clear recommendation on what to do about each one.

This is what Aster is building toward: instead of "which depot had the most claims," the question becomes "which drivers need attention, and why." The preview below uses illustrative sample profiles — also explorable from each depot's Drivers tab — not real people or real behaviour data.
